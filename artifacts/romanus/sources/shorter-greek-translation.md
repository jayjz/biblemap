## Chapter 2

1\. What occurred to Romanus on the same day at [Antioch](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01570a.htm), is also worthy of record. For he was a native of Palestine, a [deacon](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/04647c.htm) and exorcist in the parish of Cæsarea; and being present at the destruction of the [churches](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm), he beheld many men, with [women](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15687b.htm) and children, going up in crowds to the [idols](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07636a.htm) and [sacrificing](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13309a.htm). But, through his great [zeal](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15753a.htm) for religion, he could not endure the sight, and rebuked them with a loud voice.

2\. Being arrested for his boldness, he [proved](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12454c.htm) a most noble [witness](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15677a.htm) of the [truth](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15073a.htm), if there ever was one. For when the judge informed him that he was to die by fire, he received the sentence with cheerful countenance and most ready [mind](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/10321a.htm), and was led away. When he was bound to the stake, and the wood piled up around him, as they were awaiting the arrival of the emperor before lighting the fire, he cried, Where is the fire for me?

3\. Having said this, he was summoned again before the emperor, and subjected to the unusual torture of having his tongue cut out. But he endured this with [fortitude](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06147a.htm) and showed to all by his [deeds](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01115a.htm) that the Divine Power is present with those who endure any hardship whatever for the sake of religion, lightening their sufferings and strengthening their [zeal](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/15753a.htm). When he learned of this strange mode of punishment, the noble man was not terrified, but put out his tongue readily, and offered it with the greatest alacrity to those who cut it off.

4\. After this punishment he was thrown into [prison](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/12430a.htm), and suffered there for a very long time. At last the twentieth anniversary of the emperor being near, when, according to an established gracious custom, liberty was proclaimed everywhere to all who were in bonds, he alone had both his feet stretched over five holes in the stocks, and while he lay there was strangled, and was thus [honored](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/07462a.htm) with [martyrdom](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm), as he desired.

5\. Although he was outside of his country, yet, as he was a native of Palestine, it is proper to count him among the Palestinian [martyrs](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09736b.htm). These things occurred in this manner during the first year, when the [persecution](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/11703a.htm) was directed only against the rulers of the [Church](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/03744a.htm).
