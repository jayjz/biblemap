## Independence and contradictions

Kings and Isaiah share closely related passages and are one biblical textual family for corroboration purposes. Chronicles has a distinct literary presentation but should not be assumed wholly independent. RINAP 17 and 22, the Taylor Prism, palace carvings and modern catalogue descriptions belong to the Assyrian royal record family; multiple URLs do not create independent eyewitnesses. Excavation/archaeomagnetism is a different material method, though interpretation uses historical correlation. The ramp-construction hypothesis is another analytical model, not a second discovery of the ramp.

The park page calls the ramp eastern; the scholarly excavation plan locates the assault area southwest. The experience follows the scholarly plan. Posen uses a broader relief dating than the BM object records; displayed panels use the BM's narrower dates. The Lachish Letters and Babylonian destruction are deliberately excluded from the 701 BC scenes.
