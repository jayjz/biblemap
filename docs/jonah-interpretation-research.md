## Editorial controls

No direct quotes from modern thinkers enter production without primary recording/transcript, exact timestamp or page, and rights assessment. Do not imply consensus among Christian traditions, collapse typology into history, or turn observations about the psychology of flight into Jonah's documented motivation. The human creator owns all reflection questions, titles, narration, and scene interpretations.

This research does not modify Jonah scripture or draft author fields. Primary originals for patristic works are ancient; the linked English translations are later publications and should not be assumed to have identical copyright status to the originals if extensively reproduced.
