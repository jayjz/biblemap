## Editorial controls

Creative relevance determines candidate selection. Published works, reputable reprints, established translations, interviews/lectures and credible secondary collections can supply evidence; no original-manuscript or official-transcript requirement is imposed. Before publishing a quotation, verify attribution, exact wording, context and a precise page, section or timestamp against an obtainable source; prefer the original recording/work when a secondary attribution is uncertain. Clearly identify direct commentary, related argument and BibleMap's connection. Preserve translation/reproduction rights and human review. Do not imply consensus among Christian traditions, collapse typology into history, or turn psychology into Jonah's documented motive. The human creator owns final reflection questions, titles, narration and scene interpretations.

This research does not modify Jonah scripture or draft author fields. Primary originals for patristic works are ancient; the linked English translations are later publications and should not be assumed to have identical copyright status to the originals if extensively reproduced.
