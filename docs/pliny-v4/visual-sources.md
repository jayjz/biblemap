# Visual-source dossier

Bounded search to fill three visual roles; the existing V3 historical research and narrative source packet were retained. No generated imagery was needed. All selected derivatives are local, with dimensions, SHA-256 checksums, attribution, rights evidence, context and historical limits in [the manifest](../../public/media/pliny-v4/manifest.json).

[Christopher Lightfoot’s Roman Inscriptions](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/roman-inscriptions) helped locate the diploma and supports the distinction between public administrative writing, private correspondence and other inscriptional media. Its prose is not copied into the encounter.

Rejected: a Cypriot Classical/Hellenistic stylus found during the search, because its period and context were a weaker fit; modern museum photography of the Morgan manuscript, because the older public-domain publication supplied a clear reuse path. No unrelated Roman stock scenery, invented tablet, reconstructed hearing room or portrait of either woman.

Derivative policy: preserve full plate / object photograph and neutral ground, no invented details or artificial patina, no inscription cropped out. Responsive WebP at 600/1200px for the manuscript, 480/960px for the objects. Six files total, about 476KB on disk; phones select the smaller versions. Captions and provenance remain readable if images fail. Large image links work without JavaScript.

These are rights-verified visual selections with sourced draft captions, not human approval of new historical interpretation. The original V3 prose, source packets, pinned quotations, translation limits and no-index status remain unchanged.
