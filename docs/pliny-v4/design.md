## Three concepts, then a decision

| Direction | Visual language and source strategy | Reading experience | Strongest moment | Key risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The reading table — selected** | Oxide red, vellum ivory, bronze-green; large manuscript facsimile; inscribed administrative object; imperial portrait; letter locators and distinct correspondence surfaces | A continuous reading that changes form when the correspondent changes. Sources interrupt and illuminate the prose. The kept sentence docks beside the report of the two women | A private statement of obedience is brought beside an official account of coercion; the visible record then distinguishes the surviving Latin designation from absent names and testimony | Archival styling could imply a recovered dossier. Explicitly identify modern typography, later manuscript, unrelated contextual objects and common text lineage |
| Object encounters | Pale stone exhibition field; each object receives a large isolated view, with optional image inspection and short adjoining prose | Move from manuscript to household memorial culture to administrative document to coin. The objects act as the reading’s thresholds | Imperial portrait meets reported acts of worship | Objects can become a museum tour, displacing the narrative and suggesting undocumented links to specific people |
| Correspondence atlas | Geographic field, marginal letter leaves, approximate province context and sender/recipient connections | Follow correspondence between household, province and emperor, opening letters in place | The scale changes from a household boundary to imperial reach | Map movement would interrupt the intact V3 reading, and could overstate unknown hearing locations and routes |

Chosen for preserving V3’s causal and literary spine while altering its actual reading rhythm. No fourth direction or image-generation concept pass. Existing V3 prose and quotations remain unchanged. New captions and record labels are sourced editorial drafts. No new AI imagery, font, dependency, audio, animation loop or runtime source fetch.

## Composition contract

Opening: large title and intact V3 orientation beside a complete, clearly captioned facsimile. Household: an ivory letter surface, exact quotation and carry control. Province: a dark bronze-green change of documentary register, actual Trajanic diploma at the margin, request/reply correspondence. Investigation: plain readable prose with contextual imperial portrait. Gathering: spacious typographic pause. Women: oxide-red field, exact Latin designation and explicit absence register, plus reader-controlled comparison of unchanged quotations. Ending: formal imperial reply with its conditions intact. Source chain: a visible transmission explanation and complete local letters in native disclosures.

The facsimile is never a background texture. Objects retain their photographic grounds and full edges. No fake seals, simulated handwriting, theatrical redaction, invented faces or trial reconstruction. Mobile recomposes the evidence into the reading order. No-JS reading, native disclosure, focus and reduced-motion behavior remain required.
