## Three rendered alternatives

Executable composition sketches: `artifacts/james-v2/prototypes.html`. Browser screenshots: `prototype-world.jpg`, `prototype-route.jpg`, `prototype-voices.jpg`. These are deliberately quick spatial studies, not final artwork.

1. **A world, then a record — selected.** Inhabited environmental paintings precede the documentary field. Highest environmental presence; sharpest change when Josephus enters. Mobile becomes a composed vertical sequence. Two responsive local paintings, no added production dependency. Main risk: the painting may look more certain than its evidence; disclose invention on the scene and in a detailed manifest.
2. **The distance between places.** Geographic space and a path organize the testimony. Light payload, but risks an undocumented James itinerary and loses spatial clarity on mobile. Reject the path; retain explicit setting labels.
3. **A chamber of witnesses.** Names orbit an absent subject. Lightweight and strong on source distinction, but repeats V1's abstraction. Retain typographic absence for the appearance and transmission comparison for memory, rather than making it the whole story.

## Visual system

Mineral blue `#102b36`, threshold shadow `#071923`, limestone `#e8e6dd`, chalk light `#fff4e3`, water-gray `#7b9ba8`, retained memory plum `#201e29`. Existing local Playfair and Geist fonts. Type changes role: place and human scale, then isolated testimony, then documentary identification. No scroll hijack, forced waiting, autoplay or generic animation library. Native anchor cuts are deliberate changes of setting/source; motion is optional and never carries unique meaning.

The preserved narrative spine is the existing dossier's: family relationship → appearance reported without a scene → leadership and difficult fellowship → death in a political history → expanding Christian memory. No new childhood, conversion story, private dialogue, exact itinerary or death reenactment.

## Environmental research and limitations

Archaeological references and artistic references are separate in `public/media/james/manifest.json`. IAA Nazareth abstract supports domestic occupation and storage, not a James dwelling. The IAA southwest Temple Mount report supports stone courses and elevation in a particular area. The Israel Museum model represents 66 CE, later than the central James scenes, and is an artistic scale reference only. No source images were downloaded or supplied to generation. Detailed layouts, vegetation, garments, people and activities remain imagined.

Built-in image generation produced two original paintings. The initial Galilee image contained prickly pear cactus; a generation edit removed it before inclusion. Local WebP derivatives use existing `sharp`, with 768px mobile and 1536px desktop versions. The installed Next 16.1.6 implementation and [Image documentation](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/components/image) confirm `unoptimized` behavior; the project already sets it for static export. A native `picture` chooses the precompressed mobile derivative. No runtime image optimizer or paid API is introduced.

## Evaluation boundaries

Technical behavior, artistic assessment and reader outcomes are separate. Screenshots can demonstrate composition changes; they cannot demonstrate compassion, comprehension or emotional impact. An unbriefed reader study remains needed. Source and editorial release gates remain unchanged.
