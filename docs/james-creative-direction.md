# The Brother Who Remained: direction and implementation contract

## Three alternatives

| Concept | Tension and mechanism | Discovery | Risks / feasibility / mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Living Palimpsest — selected** | A person survives in overlapping, incomplete accounts. One continuous field of translucent text leaves changes from family horizon to empty opening, gathered pages, fractured record and layered memory. | Selecting a witness changes which claims survive on the same surface. Absence is visible, not filled by invention. | Must label metaphor, never counterfeit manuscript. SVG/CSS, no new dependency; simplified stacking at narrow widths. Original AI-assisted art, repository license, creator review pending. |
| **The distance between two places** | Galilee and Jerusalem anchor a restrained geographic journey; a moving reading aperture reveals regional context. | Understand spatial distance and the limits of a reconstructed itinerary. | A map risks implying travel we cannot document. Requires more geographic sourcing; small screens lose spatial relationships. Original interpretive cartography would need labels and provenance. |
| **A room of voices** | Typographic chamber: source voices appear at different distances around a central absence. | Understand who speaks, when, and in what genre. | Strongest silence around 1 Corinthians 15:7, but less continuity and physical presence; spatial effects could impede reading. Original type composition, no recordings; accessible linear fallback required. |

The selected concept combines continuity with an explicit comparison that is usable by touch and keyboard. It spends visual complexity on the relationship between sources. No forced scroll pace, autoplay, imported animation framework, or invented likeness.

## Composition

Opening: monumental James and an open, incomplete field of writing. Person, family relationship and surviving testimony are introduced before analysis. Five movements occupy the same world. The appearance removes visual detail; Jerusalem gathers separate leaves; the death record fractures the surface; memory reveals its accumulated layers. Transition changes express a change in evidence, not a camera reenactment.

Desktop: a persistent visual field beside a continuous reading column, a compact movement rail, and a source comparison that opens over the present position. Mobile: scene art above the reading flow and an independently scrollable comparison dialog. Core story and native source disclosures render as static HTML. A separate simple reading route preserves every movement and witness.

```
opening: [ James / title / invitation        | overlapping leaves ]
journey: [ movement rail                                          ]
         [ persistent evolving surface      | continuous prose    ]
compare: [ same surface / selected witness   | claim and its limit ]
ending:  [ layered memory                    | what remains known ]
```

Palette: ink plum `#201e29`, chalk `#f0e7d6`, weathered lilac `#b4a9c3`, bronze `#c5a778`, muted lapis `#798dab`, dark text `#302b30`. Playfair Display (already installed) carries names and pivotal testimony; existing Geist carries navigation and evidence. Generous left-aligned prose; no small uppercase labels or repeating cards. Lines mark source strata and sequence. The concept was revised away from literal city silhouettes: unsupported architectural detail would falsely thicken the evidence.

## Boundaries and acceptance

Reusable Human Story graph, sources, collection discovery, static-export route and no-index layout. Draft status stays in source/review disclosures. Main route `/human-story/james/`; companion `/human-story/james/reading/`. Existing story interiors remain untouched.

Verify all movement anchors, selected witnesses including empty evidence, close/escape/focus restoration, no-JS reading and source disclosures, direct links, reduced motion, blocked images, 320px reflow, 200% text and desktop/mobile axe scans. Capture 1440/768/390/320 views and inspect pixels. Automated success cannot establish reader comprehension or emotional impact.
