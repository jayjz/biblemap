## Narrative spine

John first gives Thomas words about going with Jesus in danger, then a question about the way. These recorded words establish a speaking disciple without diagnosing him. After Jesus's death, others see an appearance that Thomas misses. They report; he sets conditions. John names an interval without supplying its events. The account returns with Thomas present; Jesus addresses him; Thomas answers. No touch is narrated. The blessing turns toward people without sight; the next verses explain the written signs and their purpose. The visitor is now reading that account, without a demand to declare belief.
