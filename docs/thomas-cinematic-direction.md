## Two generated and browser-rendered directions

`artifacts/thomas/cinematic/proposals.html` renders original generated images. Desktop/mobile captures of both proposals were opened in `cinematic/studies/`.

**A · human proximity, selected.** Daylight grazes worn plaster and coarse cloth. Anonymous adults occupy the middle and foreground, without speaking mouths, named likenesses or reenacted actions. At 390px the figures retain a relationship to one another. This allows the actual biblical dialogue to do the narrative work. Material photographic detail differentiates it from James's more painterly, distant environmental scenes.

**B · enclosed nocturne, rejected as the main direction.** An oil-lit bench and large foreground shoulder are tactile, but mobile largely loses the group. The lamp is invented, adds another unverified furnishing and attracts attention disproportionate to the dialogue. The study remains a documented alternative, not a source or a scene shipped in the encounter.

The selected sequence uses three compositions: `proximity` (II), `interior` (III), `threshold` (entrance/I/V through different framing). Four generated masters, including the rejected alternative, and exact prompts are retained in `artifacts/thomas/cinematic/`. No source images were supplied to generation; no image was copied from archaeology or a film. Built-in image generation was used; the underlying model identifier is not exposed.

## Evidence and production design

[Alexandre, Atiqot 98 (2020), abstract](https://publications.iaa.org.il/atiqot/vol98/iss1/3/) was inspected for general Early Roman domestic context, including a dwelling and storage pits. It describes Nazareth, not Jerusalem or John's room. It cannot validate these layouts, furniture, pottery, clothing, textiles, doors, lighting, faces or viewpoint. All such detail is invented. No exact room, biblical conversation, supernatural body, wound or touch is illustrated. Visible forms were checked for obvious modern fixtures, glass, writing, crosses and implausible display artifacts; this is not expert archaeological validation.

The public manifest records each derivative's SHA-256, dimensions, size, original checksum, prompt, rights, source limits and reviewer. Generated-output terms were checked at [OpenAI's Content section](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/); no exclusivity or guaranteed copyright is asserted. All art remains subject to human historical and creative review. The three selected image pairs total less than one megabyte; mobile requests use 768px derivatives. No dependency is added.

## Cinematographic choices to implement and verify

1. A native, keyboard/touch/no-JS comparison control places the recorded voices together; ordinary reading remains complete before interaction.
2. Modest decorative image reframing through CSS scroll timelines, where supported; static images under reduced motion and in unsupported browsers. No text depends on animation.
3. A hard spatial cut from the inhabited room to the confession, followed by a broad daylight horizon. No forced waiting, scroll interception or representation of Jesus.

The manuscripts use a separate documentary grammar: physical witness chronology and visibly modern typeset leaves, not generated ancient writing or counterfeit facsimiles. Saying 13 is being evaluated against saying 2 because its portrayal of Thomas differs from John's public confession; the three things spoken aside are not supplied by the saying and must never be invented or offered as a reveal.
