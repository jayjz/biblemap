## Reviewable new observations

V4 foregrounds already pinned earlier speech and John 20:30–31. Connective prose describes the order of John's account, without supplying motives or a recovered emotional process. A visual interval is a modern editorial device, not evidence of lighting/weather or eight individual events.

The manuscript room will show conceptual codex/roll formats, a selected-sayings survival locator, and one bounded transcription of P.Oxy.1 verso 1–4 beside the corresponding ending of the **Coptic-based English translation**. These are not a fresh translation of Greek or a line-by-line critical collation. No missing Greek beginning is restored from Coptic. Brackets, underdots and expanded abbreviations are explained by links to the edition rather than silently imitated in invented manuscript text.

Human review remains required for all new editorial copy and the linguistic correspondence. Resemblance establishes neither apostolic authorship, dependence, historical speech nor priority. No source approvals or creator decision records are changed.
