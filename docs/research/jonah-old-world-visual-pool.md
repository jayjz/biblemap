# Jonah — Old World image curation (research candidates)

**Purpose:** Make Jonah feel situated in the ancient eastern Mediterranean and Assyrian world without pretending later art documents the events. This is a scene-directed research pool, **not an automatic image-download list**. Historical Jonah son of Amittai is connected with Jeroboam II in 2 Kings 14:25 (conventionally eighth century BCE); the biblical book's composition and dating remain debated. Never equate Nimrud with Nineveh, or portray objects of another period as eyewitness records of Jonah.

Images may come from **any source with verifiable legal reuse rights**: historical paintings, manuscripts, illustrated histories, maps, ancient reliefs, original artwork, and responsibly disclosed reconstructions. A museum is not required. Copyright and attribution still apply.

## Curation beyond institutional collections

Search **old maps and coastal engravings** for atmosphere (clearly modern interpretations when the depicted geography is reconstructed); **early Christian catacomb images and sarcophagi** for reception of the sea/fish/plant; **medieval manuscripts and woodcuts** for the book's interpretive afterlife; **public-domain paintings** for storm, prayer, and mercy; **original commissioned or BibleMap-generated compositions** for emotionally specific passage transitions. For each, locate the original object/file, date, rights holder, exact usable license, caption, and scene-specific purpose. No source-category quotas.
