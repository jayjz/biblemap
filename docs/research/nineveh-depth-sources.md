## Boundaries and candidates not used

- **Metropolitan Museum:** [*Jonah Under the Gourd, from The Story of Jonah*](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/916492), ca. 1585, **51.501.1747(4)**; after Maerten de Vos, either Antonius Wierix II or Hieronymus Wierix, published by Gerard de Jode. Object metadata directly verified. Exact image rights were not established by the retrieved page; the sitewide open-access footer is insufficient. **Rejected for embedding**, no download. CMA’s sculpture offers a stronger contrast with the clay tablet and has explicit image rights.
- **Jerome:** existing research identifies a reading at Jonah 4:1, but its English rendering is provisional and this pass did not collate an authoritative Latin edition. **Not quoted**; no unverified translation added. **Aquinas** would be a general theological lens; omitted in favor of two direct engagements. No relevant original **Wesley Huff** or **Tim Mackie** quotation was verified in this pass; neither is attributed a Jonah-specific argument.
- CMA’s inspected record does not establish an ancient findspot or original use. No claim that the marble came from a particular burial, sarcophagus or excavation. Its iconography is reception, not historical proof.
- The tablet is not a record of Nineveh’s repentance. No cuneiform translation is overlaid. Its relation to Jonah is **editorial juxtaposition**, not a claim of textual dependence.
- The two selected voices are Christian and do not represent the full Jewish, Christian or scholarly reception of Jonah. No consensus or disagreement is manufactured.
- Sculpture captions, library paraphrase, interpretive paraphrases, excerpt selection and visual juxtapositions are **provisional editorial work**. Human review is pending. The existing AI landscape and its protections remain unchanged.

## Creative choice

Three directions considered: (1) a geographical walk through supposed city locations, rejected because it would invite invented artifact locations and movement; (2) a typographic comparison of interpretations beside every verse, rejected because it would crowd Scripture; (3) optional object examination, an art viewing space and a two-voice reading, selected because each medium can disclose something different. Neither new image is mapped into the generated scene. No new historical reconstruction or narration is generated.

Acquisition URLs, creator/culture, dates, rights evidence, checksums, derivative details, attribution and limitations are in [`depth-media.json`](../../src/features/nineveh/depth-media.json). Images are held under `artifacts/nineveh-depth/media/`, outside `public/` and static export. `validate:media` checks the records and bytes. **Rights verification by Codex is not editorial approval.**
