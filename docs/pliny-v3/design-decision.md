## Selected grammar

A continuous literary reading with a movable marginal quotation. No numbered movements, progress rail, scene carousel, simulated trial, compulsory choice, audio or scroll-driven transformation. No universal story engine. The central turn places the earlier request-language and the later coercive report within one reader-controlled field while keeping separate labels and passages. The source text never morphs into different wording.

Color lock: true white `#ffffff`; cobalt `#183ac2`; reading ink `#20334f`; pale blue `#edf2ff`; coral `#b0392d`. Georgia is the literary face, the existing local Geist is for controls and source notes. Body 21–23px, 1.75 line-height, 57–64 character measure. Desktop has a reserved outer margin for the kept sentence. Mobile never reduces the artwork to a tiny desktop image or lets a full quotation become a permanent overlay.

Allowed opening copy: BibleMap; Historical witnesses; Sources; A kind of commonwealth; Pliny; Bithynia-Pontus and early-second-century context inside the prose; the final narrative opening; exact 8.16 quotation; Keep this sentence. No invented eyebrow, moral subtitle, badge, quote-shaped first-person editorial prose or generic invitation. Final review removed the small decorative entrance animation and its motion control: no motion now ships.

Container model: unframed reading column, folded marginal quotation, occasional full-width blue ground supporting a change of documentary context. Native source disclosures after the complete ending. One original generated symbolic folded blue ribbon may support the physical carrying motif; it must be separable, locally optimized, disclosed as modern AI-assisted artwork, and never described as a Roman object. No image color wash. Its failure must not remove reading or controls.

The implementation may deliberately depart from generated study copy and the prototype's artificial minimum heights, bulky mobile slip and decorative fold. These are recorded corrections, not hidden fidelity drift. Exact source language and final narrative supersede raster mockup text. Selected concept and final browser screenshots must be inspected together at 1440/390; compare content, palette, typography, carrying behavior, turning-point composition, asset treatment and mobile reflow.

## Skill decisions

- Creative direction and discovery: write complete prose first; carry a specific sentence instead of announcing an interpretation; retain community practices and release alongside suffering.
- Story Forge, pacing-doctor and prose-style-lab: compress the brigade context, remove explanation after the decisive juxtaposition, vary sentence length, end on the identified condition of pardon. No fictional interior access or invented physical action.
- Frontend app builder and imagegen: three generated concept studies, three working mechanism prototypes, selected visual inventory and explicit rejection of mockup's invented source-like text. A selected asset pass must precede production use.
- Game UI frontend: protect the reading surface. One small tool cluster; no permanent quote overlay on phones.
- Camera systems: keep the reading position stable when a quotation is kept; cut immediately under reduced motion; no camera flight or automated panning. Game-engine follow rigs are inapplicable.
- Shader programming: reject dissolve, simulated ink and pixel effects because they would add per-frame rendering without revealing a source relationship. No shader ships.
- Performance optimization: compare three fresh-context static-export samples against the WIP; budget the small interaction boundary and compressed art rather than adding a rendering loop.
- React best practices: server-rendered prose and packets; client code limited to the kept-letter state; no source module imported into the client, per-frame state or runtime retrieval.
- Source integrity and evidence contract: common-source lineage, exact quote matching, edition numbering, bounded claims, uncertainties and human-review state retained.
- Visual review and frontend testing: browser comparison at desktop/mobile, explicit distinction between readable presentation and unobserved reader response; correct three weak moments before feature freeze.
- Change verifier: review only the V3 diff against 064a17a, preserve pre-existing changes, one final validation pass with bounded failure corrections.
